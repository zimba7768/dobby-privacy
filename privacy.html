Dobby's Sense Adventures Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Dobby's Sense Adventures is a children's game developed by Seabell, based on the Dobby picture books by Alan Seabell. This page explains what information the game uses and how it is used. The short answer: it collects nothing.

Data collection

Dobby's Sense Adventures does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no in-app purchases and no account sign-in. The game asks for no permissions on your phone or tablet and does not connect to the internet while your child plays.

Information saved on your device

The game saves two small files in its own private storage on the device: the stars your child has earned in each level, and the sound and effects settings a grown-up chose. They never leave the device and are not backed up to any cloud service. You can erase the stars from the "For grown-ups" screen inside the game, or remove everything by uninstalling the game.

Links out of the game

The "For grown-ups" screen sits behind a question only an adult can answer. It has buttons that open the author's website (alanseabell.com) and this privacy policy in your web browser. Those websites have their own privacy practices.

Children

The game is made for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the game has no way to do so.

Third parties

The game is built with the Godot Engine, an open-source game engine, which does not collect data in games made with it. The game uses no other third-party services or software development kits.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
