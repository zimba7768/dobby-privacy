Dobby's Sense Adventures Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026 (purchase added)

Dobby's Sense Adventures is a children's game developed by Seabell, based on the Dobby picture books by Alan Seabell. This page explains what information the game uses and how it is used. The short answer: the game itself collects nothing, and the one optional purchase is handled entirely by Google Play.

Data collection

Dobby's Sense Adventures does not collect, share or sell any personal data, and it has no servers of its own. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account sign-in. Apart from the purchase described below, the game does not use the internet.

The one purchase

The first level, the backyard, is free. One optional purchase unlocks the other three levels and the ending. The purchase button sits behind a question only an adult can answer, and the payment itself happens in Google Play's own purchase screen. The game never sees payment details, names or email addresses. To know whether the levels should be open, the game asks Google Play whether this Google account owns the unlock, and Google Play answers with a purchase record (an order number and a confirmation code). The game uses that answer only to open the levels and to confirm the purchase to Google, as Google requires; it does not send it anywhere else. Google's handling of purchases is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information saved on your device

The game saves three small files in its own private storage on the device: the stars your child has earned in each level, the sound and effects settings a grown-up chose, and whether the full game has been unlocked (so it stays open without an internet connection). They never leave the device and are not backed up to any cloud service. You can erase the stars from the "For grown-ups" screen inside the game, or remove everything by uninstalling the game.

Links out of the game

The "For grown-ups" screen sits behind a question only an adult can answer. It has the unlock and restore buttons and buttons that open the author's website (alanseabell.com) and this privacy policy in your web browser. Those websites have their own privacy practices.

Children

The game is made for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the game has no way to do so.

Third parties

The game is built with the Godot Engine, an open-source game engine, which does not collect data in games made with it. The only other component is Google's Play Billing library, which connects the game to Google Play for the purchase. The game uses no other third-party services or software development kits.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
